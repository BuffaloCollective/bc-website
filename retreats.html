About the Experience

Every retreat is an intentional, all-inclusive four-day experience.

Before you arrive

  • Preparation materials, pre-work assignments, and virtual gatherings to help you arrive ready and connected to your Herd.
  • Health and mental fitness screening by experienced guides and licensed mental health professionals.

At the retreat

  • All-inclusive accommodation, meals, and adult beverages.
  • Morning yoga and group breakfast each day.
  • Psychedelic journey guidance from an experienced, values-aligned facilitator.
  • Dedicated time for preparation, journey, recovery, and reflection.
  • One-on-one peer support during journey days: participants divide into two groups, one journeys while the other supports, then the groups switch. No one goes through this alone.
  • Integration day: breathwork, guided reflection, and unstructured time before re-entry.

After the retreat

  • Six months facilitated Herd integration with the people who were there with you. Once a week, every week, virtual meetings to see and be seen in integration.

The retreat and the Herd are one offering. You don’t do the journey without the integration. That’s not a tagline. It’s the reason this works.
