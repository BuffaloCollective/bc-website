Why it works

Consistency

Your Herd is capped at eight and meets twice a month, every month, for a 90-minute Roundup. Plus an always-on thread between meetings. Not a drop-in. The Herd needs you the way you need them.

Relevance

Your Herd shares your values and your ambition to improve life on this planet. You don’t need a shared history to offer support and be supported. Leaders in our work, but equals in our herd.

Authenticity

Kindness, directness, no performance. Your goals, your desires, your plan to get there. You show up as you are. Healed or unhealed. Certain or not. The point isn’t to arrive already integrated. The point is to do the work with people who are doing it too.
