Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Overview

Buffalo Collective, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates buffalocollective.co. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and your choices.

What We Collect

Contact form submissions. When you fill out a contact form on our site, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, and your message.

Newsletter signups. If you subscribe to our email list, we collect your email address and any other information you choose to submit.

Analytics data. We use Fathom to understand how visitors use our site. This includes data like pages visited, time on site, referring URLs, and general location (city/region level). Fathom anonymizes IP addresses and other visitor data without using cookies and does not identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and messages
  • Send you newsletters and updates you’ve opted into
  • Understand how our website is being used so we can improve it

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Our analytics provider, Fathom, processes anonymous usage data on our behalf. Fathom’s privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy, available at usefathom.com/legal/privacy.

Cookies

Our site does not use cookies for analytics. Fathom is a cookieless analytics service, so visiting buffalocollective.co does not set tracking cookies on your device.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and newsletter subscriber information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or until you request deletion.

Your Choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send.
  • Request deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
